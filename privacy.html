Sodium Privacy

Plain-language privacy information for the community app.

What Sodium stores

Sodium stores the profile and community information members choose to add, including sessions, posts, chats, events, clip-delivery details, preferences, and issue reports. This information is used to operate the private community—not for advertising or sale.

Google Drive connection

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Sodium requests the limited drive.file permission so a filmer can choose a specific folder with Google Picker. Sodium stores an encrypted connection token and the selected folder ID so it can count completed video files. Sodium does not request access to browse the filmer’s entire Drive.

Members can disconnect Drive from the clip-delivery screen at any time. Pasting a Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, or other secure link works without connecting an account.

Sharing and outside services

Clip files remain with the outside storage provider. Sodium stores the link and delivery progress, but does not copy those Drive files into Sodium. Community uploads use Supabase and Cloudflare services needed to run the app.

Your choices

Members control what they post and share. Notification categories can be disabled in Settings. Contact the Sodium administrator from Beta Feedback in the app for a correction, deletion, or privacy question.

No ads

Sodium does not sell member information and does not use it to serve targeted ads.
