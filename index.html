Community rules

Keep the lineup good

  1. Credit the filmer. Every time.
  2. Share spots thoughtfully and respect locals.
  3. No harassment, hate, or pile-ons.
  4. Ask before sharing private details or raw footage.
  5. Use mute quietly; report serious problems to admins.
