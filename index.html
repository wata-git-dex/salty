Community privacy & consent · v1.0

Before you paddle in

One quick, plain-English agreement. No legal maze.

Why Sodium exists

Sodium is a private place to help friends surf, film, and meet up more—and spend a little less time on Instagram. It has no advertising or ad targeting.

What gets saved

We save what is needed to run Sodium: your email, profile details and photo, surf plans, posts, messages, reactions, and anything you choose to upload. Sign-in and preferences are also stored on your device.

How it is used

Your information is used to operate, protect, and improve this community. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles. Supabase and Cloudflare provide the hosting used to run Sodium.

What the crew can see

Signed-in Sodium members can see the profile information and community content you share. Treat the app like the lineup: do not post someone else's private information or footage without permission.

Surf safely

Sodium is not a forecast, emergency, or safety service. Surf plans can change. Conditions, locations, and decisions remain your responsibility.

Age and choice

Joining is optional. You must be at least 13. If you are under 18, you confirm that a parent or guardian has given you permission to use Sodium.

By continuing, you confirm that you read and understand this notice and agree to the community rules.

Sodium Community Rules

Community basics

  1. Credit the filmer, every time.
  2. Ask before sharing someone else's private details or raw footage.
  3. No harassment, hate, pile-ons, or unwanted promotion.
  4. Share sensitive spots thoughtfully and respect the people who surf there.
  5. Use mute quietly. Report serious safety or community problems to an admin.

Lineup rules

  1. The surfer closest to the peak has priority. Don't drop in.
  2. Paddle around the breaking wave, not through another surfer's line.
  3. Hold onto your board, communicate, and help when somebody is in trouble.
  4. Take turns. Don't snake, back-paddle, or treat every wave like it belongs to you.
  5. Know your limits and respect local conditions, access, and the beach.

In the spirit of Shaun Tomson's Surfer's Code: watch out for other surfers, pass along the stoke, and honor surfing.

Stokens & streaks

GET OUT THERE

Participation earns Stokens

No popularity contest. Stokens come from surfing, filming, sharing Stoke, and showing up for the crew.

REWARDS · COMING SOON

What Stokens will unlock

Member-only discounts, prints, merch, and comp entries. We’ll turn redemption on as the community grows and rewards are ready to fulfill.

The current Saltyviewfinder and WATA store discounts are available to every member—no Stokens required.

How Stokens work

+5First qualifying activity of the day

Initiated by versus added by

The person who actually made the plan receives the organizer Stokens—even if an admin entered it for them. “Added by” only records who typed it into Sodium. A nonmember can claim that credit when they join through the session invite.

Finished versus cancelled

Posting, editing, or starting a surf does not award session Stokens. When the organizer taps the red Stop surf control, the initiator and everyone participating receive their Stokens once. A cancelled surf disappears and awards nothing.

How the active streak works

Complete any qualifying activity on a new day to add one to your streak. More activity on the same day still earns its normal Stokens, but only counts as one active day.

If more than seven days pass without a qualifying activity, the next active day starts the streak over at one. The wave in the header shows your current streak.

Stokens and streaks reward real participation—not likes, followers, or scrolling.

What Sodium is for

Surfline tells you whether the waves are good. Sodium tells you what your friends are doing—who's surfing, who's filming, where, and when.

Share a surf for now or plan one ahead. Friends can join, volunteer to film, and keep the plan together without an Instagram doomscroll.

How the community works

There are no followers and no algorithm. Membership grows through invitations from people who actually surf together. Photos and clips stay properly credited, area conversations stay useful, and members choose what they share.

Built to stay simple

Sodium has no ads and does not sell member information. It is an independent community tool built by Saltyview Productions, with member data protected through Supabase.
